=== Long description (page README) ===

MPH Samus turns V1 into Samus from Metroid Prime Hunters (Nintendo DS) inside ULTRAKILL's own levels.

How to play
1. Put your Metroid Prime Hunters USA .nds file in the ULTRAKILL folder (next to ULTRAKILL.exe).
2. Start the game (the first start extracts the ROM once, about 20 seconds).
3. In a level, enable cheats, open the cheat menu (~ or Home) and turn on "Samus Mode".
   Turn on "Keep cheats enabled" to stay Samus in every level.

What you get
- MPH movement, jumping and gravity, the first-person arm cannon and its animations
- Every MPH weapon: Power Beam, Missiles, Volt Driver, Battlehammer, Imperialist, Judicator, Magmaul, Shock Coil
- All Energy Tanks and Missile / UA Expansions (799 energy, 95 missiles)
- Morph Ball with Boost Ball and bombs
- The MPH helmet HUD, effects and sounds
- ULTRAKILL rules on top: rocket-style missiles with knockback, parries with the charged Power Beam, electric
  weapons that electrify water and power switches, fire, ice, piercing shots
- Works with jump pads, lava, water, portals and gravity changes, terminals, elevators, carried items and Dual Wield
- MPH health and ammo drops, F5 third-person view

Controls: Fire shoots (hold to charge), Slide = Morph Ball, Dash = Boost Ball, 1-8 / mouse wheel / hold Q = weapons,
F = Missiles, right mouse = Imperialist zoom, E = items, F5 = third person, F4 = pointer aim on/off.

No Nintendo or ULTRAKILL assets are included: everything MPH is read from your own ROM. Fan-made, unofficial, not
affiliated with Nintendo or New Blood Interactive. Built on MphRead by NoneGiven (MIT).
